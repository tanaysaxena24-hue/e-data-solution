Common Questions About Digital Marketing Services

Here are some common questions businesses ask before starting a digital marketing project.

Discuss Your Project →
What digital marketing services do you provide? +

E Data Solutions provides SEO, technical SEO, web development, e-commerce SEO, Google Ads, social media marketing, content marketing and digital strategy services.

Can you work with an existing website? +

Yes. Existing websites can be reviewed for technical, content, usability and search related opportunities before a suitable improvement plan is created.

Do you provide SEO services for e-commerce websites? +

Yes. E-commerce SEO can include product pages, category pages, internal linking, technical SEO, content planning and search focused improvements.

How does the digital marketing process start? +

The process normally starts with a discussion about your business, goals, audience and existing digital presence. Relevant areas can then be reviewed before priorities are proposed.

Do you provide website development? +

Yes. Website development can include responsive business websites, website improvements, PHP based development and performance focused structure.

Can SEO and Google Ads be used together? +

SEO and paid advertising serve different purposes, but they can form part of the same wider digital marketing strategy when the business goals support it.

Do you provide content marketing? +

Yes. Content marketing can include website pages, blog content, SEO content and editorial planning based on audience needs and search intent.

How can I discuss my business requirements? +

You can use the contact page to share your business details, goals and the digital services you are interested in. The information can then be reviewed before discussing the next steps.
